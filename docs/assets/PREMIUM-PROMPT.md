# A friendly agent: premium artwork prompt

[Back to the index](../../README.md) · [Asset provenance](README.md)

The visual direction is a calm companion with tactile materials and a kind expression. Three objects represent tool access, durable memory and verified work. The artwork gives the directory a recognizable character while keeping the technical content readable.

## Master prompt

```text
Create a premium editorial hero illustration for Awesome AI Agents 2026.
Panoramic landscape composition, ideally 2400 × 1000, aspect ratio 12:5.
No typography, letters, words, logos or watermarks.

Design one original friendly AI companion, mature and elegant: a small
rounded pearl-white porcelain body, a translucent smoked-glass face panel,
two softly glowing turquoise eyes curved into a kind expression, a subtle
warm smile, and brushed champagne-metal details. One articulated hand is
open in a welcoming gesture; the other holds a tiny luminous amber seed,
symbolizing care and useful outcomes.

Place the companion at the right-center, with generous breathing room.
Compose just three translucent glass objects around it: an abstract tool
prism, a layered memory crystal, and a circular checkmark seal. Connect
them with thin arcs of light, like a calm constellation. Ground the scene
on a narrow sculptural platform.

Background: midnight ink-blue blending into petrol teal, restrained
volumetric haze, subtle grain, faint orbital geometry and a warm horizon.
Keep the left 35% mostly dark for an optional title added separately.
Keep the outer 10% clear for responsive crops.

Lighting: warm amber key from the upper left, soft teal rim, realistic
porcelain subsurface scattering, glass caustics and controlled reflections.
Use tactile materials, precise edges and cinematic depth of field.

Art direction: a luxury technology magazine meets contemporary industrial
sculpture. Calm, helpful, approachable intelligence at a human scale.
Palette: #071B25 midnight, #0E5960 petrol, #68D9C6 sea-glass,
#ECD7A7 champagne, #F4F0E8 porcelain.

Avoid aggressive eyes, armored humanoids, intimidating soldiers, brains,
sprawling circuit boards, neon purple, childish mascot styling, cluttered
dashboards, stock robot clipart, excessive bloom and tiny unreadable detail.
Render the finished artwork.
```
